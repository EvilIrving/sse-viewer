Privacy

SSE Inspector processes captured stream data locally in the browser.

What is accessed

After you open the SSE Inspector DevTools panel, the extension injects local observers into the tab you selected. They observe EventSource, streaming fetch and XHR responses needed to display SSE activity in the panel.

Data handling

Captured stream messages are held in the DevTools panel for the current session. They are not sent to our servers, analytics services, advertisers or other third parties. The extension uses browser storage only for its UI preferences.

Permissions

scripting injects the local observer, storage saves interface preferences and the broad host permission is necessary because developers may inspect SSE on any page they choose. The extension does not modify requests or responses.

Control and support

Close DevTools, disable or remove the extension at any time. Report privacy or security concerns through the project issue tracker.
