Privacy

SSE Inspector keeps the information it shows in your browser.

What it does

When you open the SSE Inspector panel for a page, the extension shows the real-time messages available for that page during your session.

How information is handled

Shown messages remain in your browser session. The extension does not send them to our servers, analytics services, advertisers or other third parties. Your interface choices are saved in your browser.

Your control

You can close the panel, disable the extension or remove it at any time. Report privacy or security concerns through the project support page.
